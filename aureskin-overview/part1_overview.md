# AureSkin — App Overview
### Part 1 of 2: Top-Level View

---

## What is AureSkin?

**AureSkin** is a mobile application designed to make personalized skincare accessible, intelligent, and easy to understand. At its core, AureSkin acts as a personal skin health companion — it looks at a photo of your face, understands your skin, and gives you the knowledge and tools to take better care of it.

The app goes beyond simple advice. It identifies your unique skin type and flags any active skin conditions visible in your photo, then uses that information to guide you toward the right ingredients, products, and daily skincare habits tailored specifically for you.

---

## Who is AureSkin For?

AureSkin is built for anyone who wants to **understand and monitor their skin health** over time — whether you are just beginning your skincare journey or already have an established routine you want to refine.

More specifically, AureSkin is for:

- **Beginners** who feel overwhelmed by the skincare aisle and don't know where to start.
- **Skincare enthusiasts** who want data-backed insights into their skin rather than guessing.
- **People dealing with specific skin concerns** such as acne, dark spots, dryness, or sensitivity who want to track how their skin changes over time.
- **Conscious consumers** who want to understand what ingredients are in their products and whether those ingredients are actually right for their skin type.
- **Anyone who wants structure** — a clear, personalized, step-by-step daily routine rather than a generic one-size-fits-all guide.

In short, AureSkin is for people who want to treat their skincare routine with the same care and intention they bring to other parts of their health.

---

## Navigating the App

AureSkin is organized around a **bottom navigation bar** — the five icons at the bottom of the screen that let you move between the main sections of the app at any time. From left to right, the pages are:

**Home → Library → Scan → History → Routine**

---

## App Pages

### 🏠 Home
*Your daily skincare dashboard.*

The Home page is your central hub — the first thing you see when you open AureSkin. It brings together the most relevant information for your day at a glance, without requiring you to dig through the app.

On this page you will find:
- **Your latest skin analysis result** — a quick summary of your most recent scan so you always know where your skin stands.
- **Today's skincare routine** — the steps you should be following today, pulled from your personalized routine.
- **A skin test guide tip** — a friendly reminder or helpful tip to help you get the most accurate results from your next scan.
- **Skincare articles** — helpful, real-world skincare content fetched to keep you informed and inspired about skin health topics.
- **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)** — a list of frequently asked questions about skincare and skin health.

The Home page is purely informative. It does not require any action from you — it is designed to give you a snapshot of everything important so you can start your day informed.

---

### 📚 Library *(Ingredients & Products)*
*Browse and explore a curated skincare knowledge base.*

The Library page is your in-app reference guide to the world of skincare ingredients and products. It is organized into two tabs that you can switch between:

**Ingredients Tab**
Browse a searchable collection of skincare ingredients. Each ingredient has its own dedicated page with information about what it does, which skin types it benefits, and any known concerns. This tab helps you understand what is actually inside the products you use.

**Products Tab**
Browse a collection of skincare products and their details. Each product can be viewed on its own page. If you have a product that is not already listed in the database, you can **add it yourself** directly from this tab — making the library a living, growing collection.

The Library is a learning resource built into the app, meant to help you become a more informed and confident skincare consumer.

---

### 🔍 Scan *(Skin Analysis)*
*The heart of AureSkin — your personal skin scanner.*

The Scan button in the center of the bottom navigation bar is AureSkin's main feature. Tapping it launches the skin analysis experience, where you provide a photo of your face — either taken in real time with your camera or uploaded from your gallery.

Once the analysis is complete, your results are displayed in a new page with two tabs:

**Results Tab**
This tab presents your full skin analysis. It tells you:
- Your **skin type** (one of five possible types)
- Any **skin conditions** detected in your photo (there may be more than one)
- Your **skin health score** — an overall heuristic score (0–100) summarizing your skin's visible health and barrier state
- A **summary** of what was found, written in a clear and easy-to-understand way

**Compatibility Tab** *(Ingredient Compatibility)*
This tab takes your skin analysis results and translates them into ingredient guidance:
- A list of **ingredients that are compatible** with your skin profile — the ones that are good for you
- A list of **ingredients that should not be mixed** with your compatible ingredients — helping you avoid harmful combinations

The Scan page is not just a snapshot — its results feed into the rest of the app, driving your history, your routine, and your ingredient guidance.

---

### 🕐 History *(Skin Analysis Records)*
*Track how your skin changes over time.*

The History page keeps a log of every skin analysis you have ever run in AureSkin. Your past scans are displayed as a scrollable list, each showing the date and a summary of the result.

Tapping on any past entry opens a detailed view of that scan, so you can revisit your previous skin type readings, conditions, and compatibility results at any time.

This page is particularly useful for seeing your skin's progress — whether a treatment is working, whether a new product is causing a change, or simply how your skin shifts with the seasons.

---

### 📋 Routine *(Personalized Skincare Routine)*
*Your custom skincare plan, built for you.*

The Routine page holds your personalized skincare routine — a structured, step-by-step daily plan created specifically for your skin. The routine is not generic; it is generated based on your unique skin type, your detected conditions, and your ingredient compatibility results, providing distinct morning and night regimens with specific recommended products.

When you arrive at this page, if a routine has already been generated for you, it displays your current plan in clear, ordered steps. If not, you can generate one. Once generated, you can **customize your routine by editing steps or swapping recommended products** with other suitable products from the library.

The routine functions as a clean, static step-by-step reference guide — it does not track daily step completion or send schedule notifications, allowing you to follow your routine at your own pace.

## AureSkin Features

### 🔬 Skin Analysis
AureSkin's flagship feature. When you scan a photo of your face, the app analyzes it to determine three key components:

**Skin Health Score** — an overall score (0–100) calculated using a weighted heuristic algorithm. It evaluates the combination of detected conditions, their severity and confidence levels, and skin type stability to give users an objective, trackable benchmark of their skin health over time.

**Skin Type** — classified into one of five types:
| Skin Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Normal | Balanced skin with no significant concerns |
| Oily | Excess sebum production, often shiny appearance |
| Dry | Lack of moisture, may feel tight or flaky |
| Combination | Mix of oily and dry areas on the face |
| Acne Prone | Skin that is prone to breakouts and blemishes |

**Skin Conditions** — the app can identify up to eight conditions simultaneously:
| Condition | Description |
|---|---|
| Dark Spots | Hyperpigmentation or post-acne marks |
| Inflammatory Acne | Active, red, inflamed breakouts |
| Non-Inflammatory Acne — Blackheads | Clogged open pores with oxidized sebum |
| Non-Inflammatory Acne — Whiteheads | Clogged closed pores under the skin |
| Pigmentation | Uneven skin tone or discoloration |
| Pores | Visibly enlarged pores |
| Redness | Skin flushing, irritation, or rosacea-like appearance |
| Wrinkles | Fine lines and signs of aging |

The analysis also includes **Ingredient Compatibility Analysis** — using your skin profile, the app identifies which skincare ingredients are a good match for you and which ingredient combinations you should avoid.

---

### 📖 Skincare Product & Ingredients Library
AureSkin maintains a curated database of skincare ingredients and products, accessible from the Library page. Users can browse, search, and learn from this collection at any time.

A standout feature within the Products section is the ability to **add your own products** to the database. If a product you use is not already listed, you can easily submit it through the in-app Add Product form by entering its name, brand, category, and selecting or inputting its ingredient list — making the library a living, community-driven collection.

---

### 📂 Skin Analysis Recording System
Every scan you perform is automatically saved to your personal history. The app keeps a record of your skin analysis results over time, making them easy to revisit and compare. This is the data that powers the History page, giving you a running log of your skin health journey.

---

### 🛁 Personalized Routine Generation System
Based on your skin type, detected conditions, and ingredient compatibility results, AureSkin can generate a complete, structured skincare routine tailored to your specific needs. The routine recommends specific products and steps that align with your skin profile for both morning and evening. Users have full flexibility to **edit routine steps and swap recommended products** to match what they already own or prefer. The routine acts as a static step-by-step guide without schedule alarms or completion tracking.

---

### 👤 User Profile & Accounts
Because AureSkin saves your scan history and builds a routine personal to you, each user has their own account. You can create an account and sign in to access your data across sessions, ensuring your history, routine, and preferences are always linked to you and only you.
